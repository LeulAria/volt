# 14 · Agent window UI: visual and interaction defects (round 2)

Branch `e2e2/ui` (worktree `volt-e2e-ui`), base `81563ca39e4`. Two sessions worked on it: the first
fixed the round-1 defects and a first batch (13 fixes, plus a chat-title commit and its revert); the second (after a "Sign in
to continue" stop) swept the orchestration UI. Every fix below has a before/after shot in `img/ui/`
or a measurement.

## Summary

- The five round-1 defects are fixed and re-checked at the end (Orbit labels, overlapping live
  status phrases, the stray rail glyph, the picker's mid-word path cut, "Sandbox is partial here"):
  label monitor 0 overlaps in 5 live orchestration turns, rail hidden in single-message chats, Orbit
  stops fade near the pickers and the PROJECT callout, picker rows cut at whole segments, no sandbox
  notice in any of the 7 new chats or their 14 subagent chats (session logs); the branch's 10 touched unit-test files pass (119 tests).
- Orchestration sweep: 5 live turns with two Haiku 5.5 subagents each (Tasks card, `delegate_task`,
  `wait_tasks`), covering dark/light × 100 %/120 % (Cmd+= once), with a per-frame probe for clipping,
  overlap, contrast, icon alignment and row jumps, plus a trace of what every surface says about the
  subagents. 6 defects found and fixed (19 fixes on the branch in all).
- The biggest: the composer's "N subagents running" chip appeared **25.4 s** after the subagents
  started (N=1 before) and the side list kept a finished agent until the turn ended. After: the chip
  shows **0.69 s** (median, N=5, range 0.47–1.09 s) after the first row, and chip, side list and rows
  settle within 0.53 s of each other.
- Finished subagent rows showed the model's preamble ("Running the ping command now; …") instead of
  the outcome in 2 of 4 reports; their outcome line was 2.28:1 contrast on light (3.08:1 dark), now
  4.65:1 / 6.39:1.
- Not done: council and team views could not be produced (Claude has no `council`/`spawn_team` tool
  in these chats), and chat titles still come from the model's refusal or reply (owned by the orch
  agent; this branch's attempt was reverted).

  (`/tmp/v2-ui-repo`: a throwaway git repo). 1200×800 CSS px at DPR 2. Themes "Volt Dark" and

  px), set in the profile's settings.json between runs.
- Model for live turns: `agent:seed-claude-code:claude-haiku-5-5` (Haiku 5.5, Medium) for the parent
  and both subagents (`claude-code:claude-haiku-5-5`), chats in Supervised (sandboxed, Seatbelt).
- Orchestration prompt ([`scripts/ui/orchPrompt.txt`](scripts/ui/orchPrompt.txt)): TaskCreate two
  to-dos, one `delegate_task` call with two tasks (`ping -c 20 … | tail -1`, `ping -c 12 … | wc -l`),
  `wait_tasks` until both finish, complete the to-dos, reply "done". About 45–75 s per turn.
- Drivers in [`scripts/ui/`](scripts/ui/):

    rows, the agents chip, the agents card, the side list heading and the status chip say, each
    time one changes).
  - `orchPoke.sh`: during the run, opens the Tasks card, the agents chip's menu, a hover on the side
    list's first subagent and on the first transcript row, and screenshots each.
  - `contrastOf.js`: contrast of one element over its composited background.
  - First session: `liveTurn.sh`, `overlapMonitor.js`, `statusSwapProbe.js`, `statusGapProbe.js`,
    `a11yAudit.js`.
- Load (`uptime`, 1-min) during the sweep's live turns: 15, 45, 51, 27, 50, 76 (five agents and their
  builds ran at once; the last run saw 120 on the 5-min average). Timings here are UI ordering within
  one turn, so load shifts both sides alike; turn durations are not compared.
- Raw data: [`data/ui/`](data/ui/) (`agents-chip-*.json` traces with their `uptime`, `sweep-orch*.json`
  probe output, `status-swap-*.json`). Screenshots: [`img/ui/`](img/ui/).

## Bugs found and not fixed

- **Chat titles are the model's answer** ("I can't run this. The task tools (TaskCreate…",
  "I couldn't complete this. `TaskCreate` fails schema…"), in every chat of this pass. Owned by the
  orchestration agent; 376e755 tried to drop the agent's own end-of-turn title and was reverted
  because the title comes from Volt's title call answering the message instead of naming it.
- **No council or team tool in Claude chats**: earlier attempts in this profile ("Call the council
  tool exactly once…", spawn_team) ended "no `council` tool is available". The council/team views
  could not be drawn, so they are unchecked.
- **"Waited for tasks" row while still waiting**: the transcript shows the `wait_tasks` call in its
  past-tense title above the live "Waiting · 32s" tail. Every Volt tool title is past tense and the
  tail carries the live state, so this is a convention question, not a one-row fix.
- **Same folder listed twice** in the project picker: "v2-ui-repo /Volumes/Macintosh HD/tmp" and
  "v2-ui-repo /tmp" (one folder through two paths).
- **Sidebar rows cut the project name at 8 characters** ("v2-ui-re…") with room to spare
  (`shortProjectName`, a deliberate cap).
- **Haiku called a claude.ai connector tool** (`mcp__claude_ai_Claude_Docs__guide`) in one run and
  the turn waited 2 min on its approval card until skipped. Volt passed the user's claude.ai
  connectors into a test profile's Claude; not a UI defect, worth a look by whoever owns MCP scope.
